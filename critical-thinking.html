Curriculum & Structure

Four Progressive Cognitive Defense Arcs

A systematic 466-page arc advancing from biological bugs to advanced metacognitive operational models.

PART I

The Flawed Machine

Internal neurological shortcuts, narrative fabrications, and the neurobiology of emotion (Somatic Marker Hypothesis).

  • Ch 1: Your Brain is Not Your Friend (System 1 vs. System 2, Anchoring, Confirmation Bias)
  • Ch 2: The Stories We Tell Ourselves (Narrative Fallacy, Hindsight Bias, Clustering)
  • Ch 3: Emotion vs. Reason (Motivated Reasoning, Tribal Affect Heuristics)
PART II

The Mechanics of Thinking

Formal, informal, and symbolic logic, evidence appraisal, and statistical literacy.

  • Ch 4: Logic: The Operating System of Thought (Deductive, Inductive & Abductive Proofs)
  • Ch 5: The Fallacy Files (Ad Hominem, Straw Man, Motte-and-Bailey, Whataboutism)
  • Ch 6: Numbers Don't Lie, But People Do (Simpson's Paradox, Base Rate Neglect, Relative Risk)
PART III

Information in the Wild

Navigating digital media spin, source evaluation, research methodologies, and AI chatbot defenses.

  • Ch 7: Fact vs. Opinion vs. Everything In Between (Falsifiability & Value Smuggling)
  • Ch 8: The Source Code (CRAAP Test, Lateral Reading, Incentive Triangulation)
  • Ch 9: Research Like a Detective (Citation Laundering & Source Tracking)
  • Ch 10: Detecting Manipulation and Propaganda (Digital Dark Patterns & Urgency Hooks)
  • Ch 11: The Synthetic Sycophant: Surviving AI Chatbots (RLHF Flattery & The AI Crux Test)
PART IV

The Advanced Toolkit

Systems thinking, probabilistic forecasting, constructive disagreement, metacognition, and post-mortems.

  • Ch 12: Second-Order Thinking & Unintended Consequences (Chesterton's Fence, Cobra Problem)
  • Ch 13: Probability & Uncertainty (Bayesian Updating, Expected Value, Base Rates)
  • Ch 14: Arguing Well: The Productive Disagreement (Steelmanning, Ideological Turing Tests)
  • Ch 15: The Metacognitive Edge (Premortems, Red Team Thinking, Decision Journals)
  • Ch 16: Case Studies (Challenger, Theranos, John Snow Cholera Map, Apollo 13)
  • Ch 17: The Long Game (The Productive Wrongness Cycle & Lifelong Calibration)
